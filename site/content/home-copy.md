## How it works

1. **Insert the card.** Use your Mac's SD slot or a card reader, or plug in the USB stick or drive. It appears in the list by itself.
2. **Scan.** Enter your Mac's password once, and macOS opens the disk read-only. Deleted files with their names show up first, then a deep scan keeps finding more.
3. **Recover.** Pick the files you want, or take everything, and save them to a folder on your Mac.

## Features

- **Original names first.** Reads the card's file records, so deleted files usually come back with their names, folders and dates.
- **Works after a format.** A deep scan reads every sector and finds photos, videos and documents by their contents, then names photos by the date they were taken.
- **RAW from every major camera.** Canon CR2 and CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fujifilm RAF, DNG, Olympus/OM System, Panasonic, Pentax and Samsung.
- **See results as they're found.** Thumbnails, including video frames, grouped by day, with filters for Photos, RAW, Videos, Audio and Documents.
- **Read-only and careful.** The disk is opened read-only and unmounted while scanning, and recovered files can't be saved back onto it.
- **Honest about damage.** Files with overwritten parts are marked Partial or Damaged before you recover them, and unreadable sectors don't stop the scan.

## FAQ teaser

**Is it really free?**
Yes. No trial, no limits and nothing to unlock. An optional tip is offered only after a successful recovery.

**Will it change anything on my card?**
No. macOS opens the card read-only, {{APP}} unmounts it while scanning, and recovered files are always saved somewhere else.

**Why does it ask for my password?**
macOS only lets an app read a disk directly with an administrator's permission. The password box is macOS's own, and you're asked once per scan.

**Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?**
App Store apps aren't allowed to read a memory card directly, which is what recovery needs. Download it from {{DOMAIN}} or install it with Homebrew.

**Can it recover files deleted from my Mac or iPhone?**
No. A Mac's internal SSD erases deleted data automatically and is always encrypted, and phones can't be scanned. Check Recently Deleted in Photos (kept for 30 days), iCloud.com and Time Machine instead.
