Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no trial, no file limit and nothing to unlock. {{name}} is paid for by tips from people it helped, and you're never asked before your files are back.

Will it change anything on my card?

No. macOS opens the card read-only for the app, and it won't save recovered files back onto the card. While scanning, it also stops macOS writing its own index files to the card.

Why does it ask for my password?

macOS only lets apps read a memory card's raw contents with an administrator's permission. You're asked once per scan; the app itself never gets more access than reading that one disk.

Why isn't it in the Mac App Store?

App Store apps aren't allowed to read a memory card directly, which is exactly what recovery needs. That's why the App Store versions of other recovery apps make you create a disk image in Terminal first.

Can it recover photos deleted from my iPhone?

No app can scan an iPhone for deleted photos. Open the Photos app and look in Recently Deleted (under Utilities), where they're kept for 30 days.
